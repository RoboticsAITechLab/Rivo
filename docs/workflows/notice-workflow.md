# School Notice Broadcasting Workflow

This workflow guides staff through drafting, approving, and releasing institutional circulars.

---

## 1. Notice Workflow Diagram

```
[Draft Notice Composition]
             │
             ▼
[Set Audience Cohort & Priority Level]
             │
             ▼
[Attach Official Documents / PDFs]
             │
             ▼
[Set Immediate Release OR Schedule Future Date]
             │
             ▼
[Publish -> Broadcast to Target Portals & Channels]
             │
             ▼
[Automatic Archival on Expiry Date]
```

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1: Draft Notice
1. Navigate to `/school/notices` and click **New Notice**.
2. Enter Title and Category (Academic, Holiday, Examination, General).
3. Draft the announcement text.

### Step 2: Define Audience Target
1. Select Target Audience:
   - **All School**: Visible across entire institution.
   - **Faculty Only**: Staff circulars.
   - **Specific Campus / Class**: Class-specific alerts (e.g., Grade 10 field trip itinerary).
   - **Parents Only**: Administrative circulars.

### Step 3: Scheduling & Dispatch
1. Attach relevant documentation (e.g., permission slips, schedule PDFs).
2. Choose Publication timing:
   - **Publish Immediately**: Broadcasts right away.
   - **Schedule**: Specify future release date and time.
3. Specify Expiration Date to auto-archive old notices.
4. Click **Publish Notice**.
